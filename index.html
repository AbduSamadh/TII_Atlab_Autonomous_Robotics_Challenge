Waypoint 01 — Disciplines

Two tracks,
one problem

Ground or air, the challenge is the same: perceive the environment, decide, and act — without a human in the loop. Every team competes on identical hardware, so the result comes down to algorithm quality and testing discipline.

Platform · Quanser QCar2

Autonomous
Mobility

Two vehicles share one track and race three laps. Your car has to hold the fastest line while avoiding an opponent that is also trying to win — and it has to wait for the start signal like everyone else.

Schools 14–18 Higher Ed 18–22 20 teams per level
  • ComputeNVIDIA Jetson Orin AGX · 200 TOPS
  • LidarSlamtech A2M12 · 16,000 pts · 0.2–12 m
  • VisionIntel D435 RGBD + 4× CSI at 160° FOV
  • Motion720-count encoder · 6-axis IMU
  • ToolchainPython · ROS · MATLAB / Simulink
  • Race format2 cars · 3 laps · shared track
Platform · HULA All-in-1

Autonomous
Drone

An eight-stage mission inside a 6 × 6 m netted arena. Scan a QR code to receive your route, then fly it — gates, a ball pillar, a seesaw, a precision tunnel — and land on the bullseye. No manual control at any point.

Schools 14–18 Higher Ed 18–22 20 teams per level
  • NavigationAprilTag fiducial detection
  • Camera1080p at 25 fps · 120° gimbal
  • SensingFour-way obstacle array · hybrid positioning
  • TargetingLaser emitter · AR engagement
  • Arena6 × 6 m net enclosure · 4.5 m clearance
  • Mission8 stages · takeoff to landing

Waypoint 05 — Judging

How you
are scored

Mobility — lowest time wins

Final score = average lap time + penalty time

Penalties apply for contact, illegal blocking and false starts. A clean run at a moderate pace regularly beats a fast run that collects penalties — the fastest raw lap is not the fastest result.

  • Contact+5 s
  • Illegal blocking+5 s
  • False start+10 s
  • Course departureLap void

Penalty values are indicative and confirmed in the published rulebook.

Drone — 100 points across four criteria

Mission completion40
All eight stages flown from takeoff to landing
Time efficiency25
Faster completion earns bonus points
Accuracy20
Target identification and landing precision
Dynamic adaptation15
Higher education only — response to mid-mission rule changes
